# Content and Microcopy

## Core labels

- Home actions: `Where can I go?`, `Take me there`, `I need something`, `Find a park`.
- Preference heading: `How do you like to move?`
- Primary reach action: `Show my reach`.
- Route action: `Compare routes`.
- Confidence link: `What do we know about this route?`
- Local deletion: `Delete my local data` → confirmation `Delete preferences and recent places from this device?`

## Required messages

- Location primer: `Use your location to start here. Your precise location is used for this request and is not saved on our server.`
- Denied: `Location is off. Enter a place or postcode instead.`
- Unknown step status: `We don’t know whether this section is step-free.`
- Estimated gradient: `Gradient is estimated from terrain data and may not reflect kerbs or short ramps.`
- Router failure: `We found places, but we could not check a suitable route just now.`
- No results: `No places match every selected requirement with known data. Show places with unknown details.`
- Safety: `Street conditions and facilities can change. Check signs and your surroundings.`

## Confidence terms

`Verified` = stated by a current authoritative source. `Mapped` = contributed mapping data. `Community verified` = reserved until moderated V2. `Inferred` = calculated estimate. `Unknown` = reliable information is missing or conflicting.

## Prohibited language

Do not use `safe route`, `fully accessible`, `guaranteed`, `normal walker`, `wheelchair-bound`, `suffers from`, or claim endorsement/partnership. Do not call an estimate a measurement.

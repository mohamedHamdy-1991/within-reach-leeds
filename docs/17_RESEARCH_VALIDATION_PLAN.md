# Research and Validation Plan

The app is not validated merely because it runs. Before public claims, complete:

1. Data ground-truth audit: stratified routes/parks across urban, suburban and hilly Leeds; dual-review steps, crossings, surfaces, seats and toilets.
2. Algorithm validation: compare Fastest/Easiest output against expert hand-audited routes; report false reassurance and false exclusion separately.
3. Usability/accessibility study: include older people and users of wheelchairs, scooters, walking aids and pushchairs; compensate participants; avoid collecting diagnoses unnecessarily.
4. Reliability analysis: confidence calibration by source, age and field; publish coverage and missingness.
5. Equity analysis later: compare reach distributions only with appropriate population data, disclosure control and ethics review.

Primary endpoints: task completion, comprehension of uncertainty, route suitability rating, critical routing errors and data coverage. Pre-register analysis before publishable evaluation. Do not imply Leeds Council, NHS or community groups have validated/endorsed the product unless documented.

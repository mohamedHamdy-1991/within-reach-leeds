# Scope and Success

## V1 problem

Average walking catchments hide meaningful differences in speed, stamina, steps, gradients, surfaces, crossings, toilets and places to rest. V1 makes those factors configurable and transparent without asking for diagnosis or identity.

## In scope

1. My Reach: 5/10/15/20/30-minute standard and personalised network catchments.
2. EasyRoute: compare Fastest and Easiest routes.
3. Rest Gap: seat/rest points and longest known/unknown interval.
4. Toilets: standard, accessible and Changing Places categories with provenance.
5. Services: health, Leeds Directory/community hubs and Safe Places where admissible.
6. ParkMatch: evidence-based park filters and match explanation.
7. Data Confidence: provenance, source date, coverage and unknown states everywhere.

## Not in scope

Accounts, social/community verification, leaderboards, AI, turn-by-turn/live navigation, public transport routing, live status, notifications, payments, clinical claims, policy intervention modelling, overall city accessibility score, family profiles, native apps and automatic translation.

## Success measures for pilot

- ≥80% of moderated participants complete “find an easier route” without assistance.
- ≥80% correctly identify at least one unknown/uncertain route factor.
- Median first meaningful result ≤45 seconds after consent to location or postcode entry.
- 0 critical WCAG failures and 0 known forbidden-step routes in safety fixtures.
- 100% published records have source, licence class, retrieved date and confidence state.
- Route comparison explains every penalty contributing ≥10% of extra generalised cost.

These are pilot targets, not existing achievements.

# WITHIN REACH — Leeds · Image Generation Prompt Pack

Prompt pack for generating the product's visual-identity assets. Every prompt below is
ready to paste into an image generator as-is.

## Shared identity (applies to all six images)

- **Style:** flat vector civic/wayfinding illustration. Clean geometric shapes, generous
  negative space, calm and accessible composition.
- **Palette:** charcoal/ink `#171717` (linework), paper `#F4F5F2` (background), signal
  yellow `#F4CA28` with pale tint `#FFF4BD` (accent fields), route blue `#2387C9`
  (secondary accent). No other hues.
- **Geometry:** restrained rounded corners only (10–14px equivalent radii). No sharp
  industrial edges, no bubbly softness.
- **Hard exclusions (every prompt):** no text, no letters, no numbers, no words, no
  typography of any kind inside the image; no gradients; no glassmorphism; no blur;
  no 3D; no shadows-as-glow; no wheelchair symbols; no logistics/truck/delivery
  imagery; no photographic elements.
- **Motif:** the brand mark is a small solid origin dot surrounded by two asymmetric
  nested reach contours — one dashed ink, one solid yellow — reading as "how far you
  can comfortably go."

## 1. Open Graph / social share card

**Purpose:** brand-mood share card for link previews; establishes the nested-contour
motif over an abstract map.

**Size:** 1200 × 630 px

**Prompt:**

> Flat vector civic illustration, 1200x630 pixels, no text, no letters, no numbers, no
> words anywhere in the image. A calm abstract map in pale paper tone #F4F5F2: soft
> off-white street lines, two or three muted paper-grey building blocks and one pale
> green park rectangle, all with gentle 12px rounded corners. Centered slightly left,
> a small solid charcoal dot #171717 as an origin point. Around the dot, two
> asymmetric nested reach contours that nearly fill the card: an outer contour drawn
> as a dashed charcoal ink line #171717, and an inner contour drawn as a solid signal
> yellow line #F4CA28 with a very pale yellow fill #FFF4BD at low opacity. The two
> contours are intentionally irregular and asymmetric, like a real walking catchment.
> Flat design, uniform line weights, no gradients, no glassmorphism, no blur, no 3D,
> no shadows, no wheelchair symbols, no trucks or logistics imagery, generous negative
> space in the upper right for overlay text added later.
